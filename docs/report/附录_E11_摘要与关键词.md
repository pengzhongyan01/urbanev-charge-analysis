## 中文摘要

随着新能源汽车保有量快速增长，城市充电桩的布局合理性与时段供需匹配成为充电基础设施运营的关键问题。本文以深圳 275 个交通分区、23,441 个公共充电桩的逐小时运行数据为基础，基于 Hadoop 生态搭建四层数仓完成数据清洗与汇总，并通过 Spring Boot 与 ECharts 构建充电行为分析与需求预测决策看板。分析层围绕供需匹配、价格响应、需求预测与分区画像展开：以需求份额与运力份额之差构造峰谷供需失衡指数，在 6,600 条分区—时段记录中识别出 56 个供不应求分区（占 20.4%），峰值失衡最高 0.1225，其余 219 个分区运力相对富余；在缺少分时电价标识的条件下，依据分区电价时序标准差将 275 个分区判为分时电价 78 个与固定电价 197 个，并以"晚高峰占用率减低谷占用率"度量削峰效果，两组分别为 −0.1185 与 −0.0510，方向与政策预期一致但两组夜间占用率均高于晚高峰，说明夜间慢充具有刚性；采用同周期历史均值乘趋势因子的方法预测逐小时充电量，全市汇总 MAPE 5.31%、日聚合 WAPE 1.31%，分区逐小时因单区电量尖峰重尾误差偏大（74.01%），已如实分层表述；以 7 维特征、k=4、固定种子 42 的 KMeans 将分区归为紧张型 27、过剩型 128、稳定型 79、潜力型 41 四类，紧张型平均失衡 0.0226 与失衡指数互证成立；据此按"峰值失衡 × 现有桩数 × 0.3"生成 56 条布点建议、合计新增 93 个桩，并发现缺口时段集中在凌晨至上午。最后以双口径电量校验（全市偏差 58.72%）提示桩侧与车侧电量口径差异，为"哪里建桩、何时错峰"提供量化依据。

## Abstract

With the rapid growth of electric vehicle ownership, the rationality of charging pile deployment and the temporal supply–demand matching have become key issues in charging infrastructure operation. Based on hourly operating data of 23,441 public charging piles across 275 traffic zones in Shenzhen, this paper builds a four-layer data warehouse on the Hadoop ecosystem for data cleaning and aggregation, and develops a charging behavior analysis and demand forecasting dashboard with Spring Boot and ECharts. The analysis layer addresses supply–demand matching, price response, demand forecasting and zone profiling. A peak–valley imbalance index is defined as the difference between a zone's demand share and its capacity share; among 6,600 zone–hour records, 56 zones (20.4%) are identified as supply-deficient with a maximum peak index of 0.1225, while the remaining 219 zones hold surplus capacity. Without an explicit time-of-use tariff flag, zones are classified by the standard deviation of their electricity price series (78 TOU vs 197 FLAT), and the peak-shaving effect is measured as the gap between evening-peak and night-valley occupancy, yielding −0.1185 for TOU and −0.0510 for FLAT zones: the direction matches policy expectations, yet both groups show higher overnight occupancy, indicating rigid night-time slow charging. Hourly demand is forecast by a same-cycle historical mean multiplied by a trend factor, achieving MAPE 5.31% and daily WAPE 1.31% at city level, while zone-level hourly errors (74.01%) are reported and explained by spiky, heavy-tailed low-volume series. KMeans with seven features, k = 4 and a fixed seed of 42 groups the zones into tight (27), surplus (128), stable (79) and potential (41) clusters, and the tight cluster's mean imbalance of 0.0226 corroborates the index-based ranking. Based on peak imbalance × existing piles × 0.3, 56 expansion suggestions totalling 93 piles are generated, revealing that gaps concentrate in the small hours rather than at the evening peak. A dual-caliber energy check (58.72% city-level deviation) further highlights the difference between pile-side and vehicle-side metering, providing quantitative support for where to build piles and when to shift charging demand.
